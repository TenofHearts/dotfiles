Hyprland shortcuts

In every mode, Alt + H/J/K/L focuses left/down/up/right. On a Monocle workspace, Alt + H cycles to the previous tiled window and Alt + L to the next, wrapping at either end. Alt + 1-9 switches workspaces. Alt + M asks the focused window to close. Alt + Q force-kills its process without a save prompt.

Escape or Enter leaves any mode. In Command, press ? for this guide or Alt + Shift + ; to return to Normal.

Join, minimize, pause-management, and accordion bindings are intentionally unassigned because Hyprland has no loyal equivalent for them.
